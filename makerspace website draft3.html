Welcome!

We provide affordable access to tools, classes, workspace, and a community of makers in the heart of Montgomery County, Maryland

Visit Us

Come in during Member Hours on Saturdays or Tuesdays to see what we have to offer

Sign up for a Class or Event

An excellent way to learn new skills and to meet other people, we offer a wide range of classes throughout the years for adults and kids of all skill levels. Many classes are open to nonmembers but free or reduced price for members.

Become a Member

Members can use the makerspace for projects, borrow tools from the tool library, take free and discounted classes, and bring one guest at a time.

What we do

Makerspace members and their guests can use a lot of things (some items require training and/or fees). Nonmembers are also welcome to use the seed library for free.

3D printing

Members can use 3d printers, 10 cents per gram. Bring in an .STL file and we'll help you print it. Many colors of PLA available. Other types of filament rare.

CNC machine

computerized carving of wood and other materials. training and certification required.

Crafting and Cosplay

Foamcutters, glueguns, paint, button maker and more!

Cricut

Cricut maker 4 with accessories. Adhesive vinyl and cardstock available for use.

Laser engraving & cutting

for cloth, wood, other materials. Filter fee of $10 for the first hour, then $5 per half hour.

Electronics

Soldering tools, oscilloscopes, power supplies, and more

Embroidery Machine

Requires training. Embroidery designs into cloth!

Seed Library

Available for free for everyone. Bring some seeds in to share with others and take what you need.

Sewing

Multiple sewing machines available. We also have an embroidery machine and serger, both require training class to use.

Silkscreening and Heat Transfer

Silkscreen press with multiple screens and heat presses for permanently putting designs on shirts, bags etc.

Tool Library

Borrow tools and other items for 2 weeks at no additional cost. May be renewed once. Late fees accrue at $1/item/day.

Woodworking

Safety training and certification required. Bandsaw, scrollsaw, drillpress and sander.
